Everything ChatGPT does — one loop.

§ 06.2 THE DISTRIBUTION OF THE NEXT TOKEN

A Gaussian.
Or maybe a Poisson.

The model is a probability distribution over the next token — your knowledge, your language, your style, compressed.

The honest math:
p(wₜ | w<ₜ)

That's it. That's the whole game.
